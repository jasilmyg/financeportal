Page 1 of 8

The Dashboard Hub

  • What it Contains: Key metrics tiles (Value Penetration, Volume Sourcing, Disbursement Aged Pendency, Contest counts) and visual target graphs showing targets vs. achievements.
  • Sourcing Import Dropzone: Allows quick CSV dragging to auto-fill the active review tables.
  • Control Panel: Global buttons to save inputs, export the Excel workbook, or trigger PowerPoint generation.

The Logic & Business Idea

Acts as the executive summary cockpit. Instead of digging through multiple spreadsheet tabs, upper management gets an instant snapshot of financier performance, pending payouts, and contest statuses for the week.

High-level Health Check
Page 2 of 8

The Exec Summary Tab

  • What it Contains: Detailed matrixes including the Q1 Target Achievement Status and Monthwise Performance (Q1) grids.
  • Automated GAP Projections: Compares penetration percentages (Volume, Value, Cards) against baseline targets and shows the deficit (GAP) in Crore.
  • Monthly Trends: Tracks Apr, May, and Jun values to evaluate trajectory.

The Logic & Business Idea

Focuses on strategic gap analysis. It helps the finance team answer: *How far are we from our quarterly volume and card penetration targets?* Highlighting the gap helps redirect focus to drive promotional activities (e.g., zero down-payment schemes) mid-month.

Strategic Gap Targeting
Page 3 of 8

Disbursement & Sourcing

  • What it Contains: Sourcing and disbursement pending statistics grouped by financier (Bajaj, HDB, IDFC, TVS, DMI) and the **Top-5 Stores Disbursement Pending (High Aged)** list.
  • Pending %: Auto-calculates what percentage of sourced loans are stuck in processing.
  • Aged counts: Highlights files stuck for multiple days.

The Logic & Business Idea

Focuses on working capital optimization. Sourced loans are just numbers until the cash is disbursed by finance partners. By identifying financiers with high pending rates and stores with aged files, management can push relevant partners to release funds faster, boosting cash flow.

Cash Flow & Payout Speed
Page 4 of 8

Category Performance

  • What it Contains: Financier-wise categories matrix mapping counts across CD Products, HVA, Laptop, Mobile, SA, and Others.
  • Financier Split: Shows which financing partners own the majority share in specific product segments (e.g. Bajaj dominates Mobile finance, HDB dominates CD Products).

The Logic & Business Idea

Focuses on product-finance compatibility. Helps identify where to introduce financier subventions (e.g., zero-interest EMI schemes on laptops). If laptop penetration is low, you can negotiate subvention schemes with the leading financier to boost sales.

Finance Penetration Map
Page 5 of 8

Special Contest Tracker

  • What it Contains: Contest target matrices (such as the DMI Samsung Contest) detailing targets, achievements, and percentage completions by store or region.
  • Live tracking: Tracks volume counts against strict deadlines set by brands or finance companies.

The Logic & Business Idea

Focuses on maximizing partner payouts (incentives). Financiers like DMI frequently run high-reward contests. Keeping targets front and center ensures that stores actively pitch relevant financed products, allowing the brand to secure higher bulk commissions and bonuses.

Incentive Maximization
Page 6 of 8

Manpower Status Tab

  • What it Contains: Active counter of on-ground financier counter staff (e.g. Bajaj, HDB, IDFC representatives) across stores, and their corresponding conversion rates.
  • Productivity Index: Compares total store footfall/invoices against counter staff conversion counts.

The Logic & Business Idea

Focuses on resource efficiency. If a store has three finance counters but low sales, or if a single representative is generating massive volume elsewhere, management can request financiers to reallocate manpower to maximize conversions.

On-Counter Manpower Productivity
Page 7 of 8

The Data Cleaner Page

  • What it Contains: Drag-and-drop file upload zones for raw CSV imports, and the **Combined Sales Report Cleaner & Exporter** zone.
  • Success Banner: Displays counts of successfully cleaned sales records instantly.
  • Direct Download: Download button to retrieve the fully formatted, relinked Excel summary workbook.

The Logic & Business Idea

Focuses on operational automation. Eliminates the weekly headache of manual spreadsheet sanitization. The cleaner parses rows, drops invalid sales, maps categories, and fixes broken formulas—saving hours of work.

Zero Manual Cleaning
Page 8 of 8

Global Targets configuration

  • What it Contains: Configuration inputs for monthly target parameters, target penetration rates, and target financier margins.
  • Config Sync: Updates the backend configuration in real-time, immediately recalculating all dashboards and charts.

The Logic & Business Idea

Acts as the baseline parameters configuration. Without a flexible baseline config, targets would have to be hardcoded in scripts. This page allows the finance team to adjust targets month-on-month as targets scale up or down.

Flexible Baseline Parameters

Performance Impact

Slashes weekly execution time from **4 hours to 10 seconds**. Minimizes human entry errors to 0%, ensuring accurate calculations and seamless presentations for leadership meetings.
